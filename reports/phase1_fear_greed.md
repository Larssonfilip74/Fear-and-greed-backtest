**Result: CNN revises published values for about 40 sessions, then they are final** (0 revisions among 125870 pairs older than 40 sessions). A live trader acts on the first print, while history holds the settled value. The first print's error is typically ≈1–3 points (max 5.5). This is a hidden look-ahead channel, handled by protocol amendment A1 (§11).

Holiday rows are dropped. A removed real session is a missing value (never filled for more than 3 sessions).
